# Strateo

**Strategy + intelligence + process = results.**

Consulting in business processes, management, regulation, and AI automation. We organize priorities, routines, indicators, and controls to turn decisions into operational practice.

Based in Goiânia, Brazil, we work nationwide with investment advisory firms, financial and capital markets businesses, service companies, agribusinesses, and family businesses.

## How we work

- **Processes and management** — Sales and management routines with clear ownership, cadence, and indicators.
- **Regulatory consulting** — Regulatory and self-regulatory requirements translated into policies, controls, responsibilities, and evidence.
- **AI automation** — Automation of repetitive steps within processes with defined rules and control points.

Our method combines **field diagnosis → routines and cadence → indicators and control → AI automation**, with implementation and follow-through in daily operations.
